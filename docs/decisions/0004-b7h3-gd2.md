# 0004 — B7-H3 reagents are not merged; B7-H3 and GD2 are excluded from scores

Status: Proposed (2026-11-19)

## Context
Two batches measured B7-H3 with different reagents, and step 00 merges them into one
column. B7-H3 and GD2 intensities differ by more than an order of magnitude between
batches, and the batches are not balanced across the groups being compared.

## Decision
- The two B7-H3 columns stay separate (`B7H3_CD276`, `CD276_B7H3`); step 00 writes a table
  of every name merge it performs, for review.
- B7-H3 and GD2 are excluded from the functional scores and from comparisons across
  batches until the reagent of each column is confirmed.

## Consequences
Scores that used them (tumour immune evasion, tumour antigen load) are recomputed
without them. Other merged names (TIM-3, NKG2D) stay merged until the reagent is confirmed.
